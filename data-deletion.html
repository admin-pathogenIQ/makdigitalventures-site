Data Deletion Request

Last updated: May 2026

Mak Digital Ventures allows users to request deletion of personal data associated with our apps, including World Cup 2026 Hub, without deleting their full app account.

Request deletion of specific data

You may request deletion of specific app data such as profile details, favorite teams, chat messages, reports, notification preferences, or other app activity data.

To request deletion, email us at: support@makdigitalventures.com

Please include:

  • The app name
  • The email address used in the app
  • The type of data you want deleted
  • A short note saying you want data deletion without deleting your account

Account deletion

If you want your full account and associated app data deleted, you may also request full account deletion by emailing the same address.

Processing time

We will review and process deletion requests within a reasonable time, unless we are required to retain limited information for legal, security, fraud prevention, or compliance purposes.
